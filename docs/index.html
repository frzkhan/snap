Snap

A tiny screenshot tool for your Mac. Press a shortcut, select an area, draw an arrow or a box, then copy or save. About 13 MB of memory.

Features

Your own shortcut

Starts as ⇧⌘2. Change it to anything in Settings.

Draw on it

Arrows, circles and rectangles in five colours, with undo.

Copy or save

Copy to the clipboard, or save a PNG straight into a folder you choose.

Really lightweight

About 13 MB of memory and a download under 1 MB. Lives in the menu bar only.

Sharp on every screen

Works across multiple displays at full Retina resolution.

Open at login

Turn it on once from the menu bar and it's always ready.

Install

  1. Click Download for Mac above and unzip Snap.zip.
  2. Drag Snap.app into your Applications folder and open it. A camera icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. The first time you take a screenshot, macOS asks for permission. Turn on Snap in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, then press the shortcut again.
If macOS says Snap can't be opened: Snap isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. Or run this in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Snap.app
